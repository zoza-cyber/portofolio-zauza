## Brand & Style
Obsidian Archive merges high-precision administrative rigor with brutalist technical aesthetics and futuristic digital tailoring. The personality is disciplined, authoritative, yet playfully kinetic—reflecting institutional efficiency paired with young, razor-sharp technical execution.

Visual aesthetics blend **Neo-Brutalism**, **Technical Monospacing**, and **Subtle Dark Skeuomorphism**:
- Deep obsidian backgrounds punctuated by hairline technical borders (`rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.12)`).
- High-contrast typography featuring massive condensed display headlines, utilitarian sans body copy, and mechanical monospaced metadata brackets.
- Archival folder cues (notched clip-path folder tabs, filing classification numbering like `[ 01 / PROFILE ]`).
- Interactive tactile feedback utilizing spring physics and energetic cyan electric accents (`#38BDF8`).

## Colors
The color architecture relies on a dark monospaced technical framework:
- **Base Neutral (Obsidian `#111111`)**: Deepest ground canvas creating infinite depth.
- **Surface Tiers (`#151515`, `#1A1A1A`, `#242424`)**: Functional elevation layers for containers, archival cards, and hover states.
- **Text Tiers**: Pure contrast with `ink` (`#F4F4F2`) for primary readings and metallic silver gradient treatments on oversized heroes, balanced against `ink-muted` (`#9A9A96`) for secondary details.
- **Accent (Electric `#38BDF8`)**: High-visibility technical cyan reserved for active indicators, status dots, interactive states, brackets, and primary action highlights.
- **Status (Emerald `#10B981`)**: Subtle live availability pulse ping.

## Typography
The system enforces strict tripartite typographic assignments:
1. **Display & Section Titles (Display / Syne)**: Heavy, structural, unapologetic capital lettering. Hero elements scale responsively with viewport width units (`13vw`–`15vw`) and feature linear metallic vertical gradients (`#FFFFFF` to `#8E96A0`).
2. **Body & Reading (Plus Jakarta Sans)**: Highly legible, neutral sans with open apertures ensuring clear reading across long administrative accounts and narrative cards.
3. **Data & Metadata (JetBrains Mono)**: Monospaced labels, index notations (`[ 01 / PROFILE ]`), telemetry coordinates, and status ribbons set in uppercase with extended letter-spacing (`0.14em`).

## Layout & Spacing
- **Grid Architecture**: Built on a max-width container (`80rem` / `1280px`) with symmetrical outer safety gutters starting at `1.5rem` on mobile and scaling to `2.5rem` on desktop.
- **Section Rhythm**: Sections are separated by standardized `border-b border-line` dividers with generous vertical padding (`6rem` / `96px`), creating distinct chaptered pacing.
- **Multi-column Schemes**: Asymmetric 12-column layouts (`col-span-7` editorial narrative / `col-span-5` data telemetry) and balanced 3-column bento matrices.
- **Background Grid**: A repeating subtle dot grid (`radial-gradient(rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.08) 1px, transparent 1px)` on `28px 28px` pitch) anchors technical alignment.

## Elevation & Depth
Depth is created structurally rather than with drop shadows:
- **Low-Contrast Ghost Outlines**: Strict structural boundaries using `border border-[rgba(255,255,255,0.12)]`. Surfaces lift on hover by tinting borders directly to electric blue (`#38BDF8`).
- **Surface Layering**: Stacking `obsidian (#111111)` base canvases below `surface (#1A1A1A)` panels and `surface-hover (#242424)` interactive cards.
- **Backdrop Diffusion**: Navigation employs `backdrop-blur-md` with `obsidian/85` opacity to allow scrolling content silhouettes to filter through gently.
- **Glow & Highlights**: High-impact interactive assets (such as character avatars) utilize low-opacity ambient back-glows (`rgba(56, 189, 248, 0.10)` expanding to `0.25` on interaction) and heavy localized drop shadows (`drop-shadow-[0_24px_48px_rgba(0,0,0,0.85)]`).

## Shapes
The design embraces a strictly angular, mechanical identity:
- **Default Geometry**: `0px` border-radius (`roundedness: 0`) across cards, input containers, bento modules, and buttons.
- **Archival Folder Notching**: Folder tabs utilize custom geometric polygon clipping: `clip-path: polygon(0 0, calc(100% - 14px) 0, 100% 100%, 0% 100%)`.
- **Exceptions (Pills & Badges)**: Status badges, ticker capsules, and live indicator dots use pure circular or pill-shaped geometry (`rounded-full`) to contrast against the sharp rectangular architecture.

## Components

### Buttons & Interactive CTAs
- **Primary Action**: Solid `ink` background (`#F4F4F2`) with `obsidian` (`#111111`) bold monospaced uppercase typography. Transitions on hover to electric blue (`#38BDF8`). Includes directional arrows or status indicators (`↓`, `↗`).
- **Secondary Action / Ghost**: Transparent or `surface` background bordered with `line` (`rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.12)`). On hover, text and borders turn `electric`.
- **Copy Utility Buttons**: Surface button with embedded monospace label, interactive icon prefix (`✉`), and dynamic inline feedback token (`[COPIED!]`).

### Archival Folder Cards
- Structured dossier appearance with an absolute offset folder-tab pill (`folder-tab`) along the top-left edge carrying numbering metadata (`01 // INTERNSHIP`).
- Divided internally with faint hairline horizontal rules (`border-line/60`).
- Three-column breakdown for contextual highlights, indexed with electric four-point stars (`✦`).

### Badges & Metadata Chips
- Monospace font (`JetBrains Mono`, `10px` / `11px`), full uppercase, tracked out (`0.14em`).
- Framed in black (`#111111`) with hairline white borders.
- Brackets wrap category indices: `[ 01 / PROFILE & BACKGROUND ]` in `electric` accent color.

### Ticker & Continuous Marquee
- Seamless horizontal scrolling marquee bounded top and bottom by hairline dividers.
- Items partitioned by electric star glyphs (`✦`) at regular intervals.
- Pauses smoothly on `:hover`.

### Status Indicators
- Twin-circle ping indicator with a core emerald dot (`bg-emerald-500`) enveloped by a continuous pulse ring (`animate-ping bg-emerald-400`).
